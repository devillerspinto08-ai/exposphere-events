About ExpoSphere

Creating Impactful Platforms.
Where Every Moment Matters.

ExpoSphere Events Private Limited is a Mumbai-based experiential events, exhibitions, entertainment, and brand activation company dedicated to creating impactful platforms that connect businesses, brands, industries, and audiences.

We specialize in conceptualizing, planning, organizing, and executing premium events across multiple industry segments. Our approach combines creativity, strategic planning, innovative technology, professional execution, and audience engagement to deliver memorable experiences and measurable business outcomes.

Our vision is to establish ExpoSphere as one of India's leading experiential events and exhibition companies, building scalable event properties and creating sustained opportunities for businesses, sponsors, exhibitors, investors, and communities.
