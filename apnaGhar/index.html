About The Exhibition

More Than an Exhibition.
A Complete Homeownership Experience.

APNA GHAR is a dedicated Real Estate & Home Ownership Exhibition conceptualised by ExpoSphere Events Private Limited. The exhibition is designed to bring property developers, homebuyers, financial institutions, real estate consultants, interior designers, and allied home solution providers together on one integrated platform.

Whether you are searching for your first home, upgrading your lifestyle, investing in real estate, or exploring property-related services, APNA GHAR provides an opportunity to connect directly with the right people and explore multiple solutions under one roof.

Our objective is simple: To make the homebuying journey easier, smarter, and more accessible.
